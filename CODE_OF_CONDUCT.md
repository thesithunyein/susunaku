# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be decent. This is a small open-source project about money that people need; the community
should be somewhere a first-time contributor is glad to arrive.

Specifically:

- **Assume competence and good faith.** Questions are welcome; so are mistakes.
- **No harassment, insults, or personal attacks.** Disagree with the code, not the person.
- **No spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or crypto-scam behaviour.** This project
  exists in a space full of grifters; we do not feed that pattern.
- **Respect that this is money software.** Claims about what the app can or cannot do get
  checked against the ledger before they get argued.

## Scope

This applies to the repository (issues, PRs, discussions) and any project-run space
(demo calls, the hackathon booth).

## Enforcement

Report problems to **sithunyein.mailto@gmail.com**. Reports are handled within a week.
Consequences, in escalating order: a private warning, a temporary block from project spaces,
a permanent ban. Anything illegal gets reported where the law says it must be.

Maintainers are held to this standard too — stricter, actually, because they hold the merge
button.

This text follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org)
without copying its boilerplate.
